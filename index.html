# Privacy Policy — CartSync _Last updated: April 18, 2026_ CartSync ("the App") is a Shopify app operated by [YOUR NAME/COMPANY] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App processes and why. ## 1. Data we collect When a merchant installs CartSync on their Shopify store and a signed-in customer interacts with their cart, we process: - **Shop domain** (e.g. `example.myshopify.com`) — to associate data with the correct store. - **Customer ID** — Shopify's internal identifier for a signed-in customer. We do not collect customer names, emails, or addresses. - **Cart contents** — product variant IDs and quantities that the customer has in their cart. - **Device identifier** — a random UUID generated in the customer's browser (stored in localStorage) to distinguish devices for the same customer. - **Theme cart selectors** — CSS selectors detected from the merchant's theme to refresh cart UI elements after a restore. We do **not** collect payment information, customer personal details, order history, or any other customer data beyond what is listed above. ## 2. How we use the data We use the data only to provide the App's functionality: saving a signed-in customer's cart on one device and restoring it when the same customer signs in from another device or returns later. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties. ## 3. Data storage and retention Cart data is stored in a secure database managed by us. Data is retained while the App remains installed on the merchant's store. When the App is uninstalled, we delete all data associated with the merchant's shop within 30 days, triggered automatically by Shopify's `app/uninstalled`, `shop/redact`, and `customers/redact` webhooks. ## 4. Compliance webhooks CartSync subscribes to Shopify's GDPR/CCPA compliance webhooks: - `customers/data_request` — we respond with the data we hold about the requested customer, if any. - `customers/redact` — we delete data associated with the requested customer. - `shop/redact` — we delete all data associated with the requested shop. ## 5. Your rights Customers whose data is processed by the App can: - Request a copy of the data we hold about them. - Request deletion of their data. Requests are made through the merchant's store, and the merchant forwards them to Shopify, which relays them to us via the compliance webhooks above. ## 6. Security Data is transmitted over HTTPS and signed requests via Shopify's App Proxy. Only authenticated requests from Shopify are accepted by our servers. ## 7. Contact For any questions about this policy, contact [YOUR EMAIL]. ## 8. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
